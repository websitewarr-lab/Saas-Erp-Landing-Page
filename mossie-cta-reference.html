This standalone concept includes your four requested fields: name, mobile number, email and a module dropdown. The dropdown supports multiple modules or the whole platform.

  • Company name and team size: inspired by Odoo’s contact form, which asks for company and company size. Both are optional here to keep the form quick.
  • A demo around your workflows: inspired by Zoho ERP’s demo page, which discusses business processes and specific requirements. Mossie’s module selection makes that intent easy to express.
